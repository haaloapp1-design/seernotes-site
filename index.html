Writing

Write anything, from a quick line to a long document

A new note is one tap away, and every change saves itself as you type.

  • Rich text: headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight, colours, quotes, dividers, links and code blocks.
  • Bullet, numbered and tick lists, plus simple tables.
  • Type / at the start of a line for a quick menu of blocks, and [[ to link another note.
  • Templates for meetings, study notes, projects, daily plans and weekly reviews, or save any note as your own template.
  • Fold long sections under their heading, with undo and redo.
  • Focus mode hides everything but your words; reading mode makes long notes easy on the eyes.
  • Note info: word count, reading time, created and edited dates.
  • Choose the editor font, text size, line spacing and page width.
  • If the app ever closes mid-sentence, your last edit is restored.
Checklists

Lists that get things done

Shopping, packing, projects: tick things off and watch the progress bar fill.

  • Checklist notes, or tick lists inside any text note.
  • Ticked items move to the bottom; drag to reorder.
  • Give any item a date and time to see it on Today and get a reminder.
  • Lists that reset themselves on a schedule, like a weekly review every Friday.
  • Progress on the home card, for example “2/5 done”.
Today & reminders

Your whole day in one place

Today gathers every task with a date, from every note, alongside your habits.

  • Overdue, due today and upcoming tasks, grouped by note. Tick one and the original note updates.
  • A month calendar with your notes and tasks on each day.
  • Reminders on any note: once, daily, on weekdays, weekly on chosen days, monthly or every few days.
  • Snooze or mark done right from the notification.
  • A weekly progress card: tasks done and days you wrote in your journal.
  • Notification privacy: show the note title, or just “Seer Notes reminder”.
Journal

A journal that knows your days

One entry a day, with gentle prompts and a mood, in its own tab.

  • A daily prompt (over 60 built in, in English and Urdu), with shuffle, or add your own.
  • Five moods shown as calm colours, never emoji, plus photos, voice and a place.
  • Journal templates: three gratitudes, daily reflection, learned today, evening review.
  • Streaks, a mood calendar for the month and “On this day” from past years.
  • Lock the journal with your fingerprint, separately from the app lock.
  • An optional daily reminder at a time you pick.
Voice notes

Say it, and find it later

Record a thought, a lecture or a meeting. Speech becomes searchable text on your phone.

  • Big record button with a live waveform, pause and resume.
  • On-device speech to text in English or Urdu, where your phone supports it.
  • Keeps recording with the screen off, with a notification to stop.
  • Drop bookmarks while recording or playing, and jump back to them.
  • Type notes during a recording; tap a paragraph to hear that moment.
  • Playback from 0.75× to 2×, skip back 10 s or forward 30 s, and trim without changing the original.
Photos, scans & PDFs

Text in your pictures becomes searchable

Snap a receipt, a whiteboard or a page of a book. Seer Notes reads the text on your phone, with no internet.

  • Add photos from the camera or gallery; crop, rotate, reorder and caption them.
  • Copy text straight out of a photo.
  • Scan documents with automatic edges, perspective fix and filters, into clean multi-page PDFs.
  • Every scanned page is read, so search finds the words inside.
  • Attach any file. PDFs open inside the app.
  • Annotate PDFs: highlight, draw, comment and bookmark pages. The original file is never changed, and you can export an annotated copy.
Sketch & handwriting

Draw it, write it by hand, turn it into text

A sketch block inside any note, made for fingers and styluses.

  • Pen, pencil, highlighter and eraser, with colours and widths.
  • Undo and redo, and lasso to move or resize what you drew.
  • Smooth strokes, with stylus pressure where your phone supports it.
  • Turn handwriting into typed text on your phone, after a one-time language download.
  • Handwritten words are found by search.
Search

Find anything as you type

Results update with every letter, across notes, checklists, photos, scans, handwriting and voice transcripts.

  • Matches are highlighted, and a result opens at the right paragraph, page or moment of a recording.
  • Filter by type, tag, notebook, colour, favourite, attachment and date.
  • Power search: "exact phrase", -exclude, tag:work, type:voice, has:pdf, edited:7d and more.
  • Pin a search to the home screen as a chip.
  • Recent searches, one tap away.
Notebooks & links

Organised your way, connected like your thoughts

Use as much or as little structure as you like.

  • Notebooks with folders inside folders, colours and icons.
  • Tags, including nested tags like work/budget.
  • Pin and favourite notes; archive what you're done with.

Study

Spaced repetition shows each card again just before you'd forget it.

Health records

Your family's doctor visits, in order

Keep everyone's visits, medicines and prescriptions together, for yourself, your children and your parents. Free for everyone.

  • A timeline for each person: date, doctor, clinic, symptoms, diagnosis and notes.
  • Medicines with dose, how often and for how many days, with a “Taking now” list.
  • Photos or scans of prescriptions, reports and bills, with the text in them made searchable.
  • Health basics at a glance: blood group, allergies, long-term conditions and an emergency contact.
  • Follow-up reminders that only say “Follow-up visit” and a name, never medical details.
  • Share a person's history, or one visit, as a tidy PDF for a new doctor.
  • Lock Health records with your fingerprint or app PIN.

Health records are kept apart from your notes: they never appear in home, search, widgets or notifications, and are never sent to AI. Seer Notes keeps records; it does not give medical advice.

Vault & app lock

A private vault for what matters most

Move any note or folder into the Vault. It's encrypted on your phone and opens only with your fingerprint or vault PIN.

  • Vault notes and their attachments are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key kept in Android's secure hardware.
  • They never show in home, search, widgets, notifications or the app switcher, and are never sent to AI.
  • Locks itself after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or as soon as you leave the app.
  • App lock for the whole app with fingerprint, face or PIN, and an option to hide content in recent apps.

There is no back door. If you forget the vault PIN and lose fingerprint access, vault notes can't be recovered, by you or anyone else.

Backup & export

Your backups, in your storage

Keep a copy in case you lose your phone, in places you own, not ours.

  • Back up to this phone, a folder or SD card you pick, your Google Drive (a hidden app folder only Seer Notes can see) or a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud. Dropbox and OneDrive are coming.
  • Encrypted with a password only you know (AES-GCM with Argon2id).
  • Automatic daily backup, with a Wi-Fi only option, and “Back up now”.
  • Choose how many versions to keep, and see each one's size.
  • Restore everything, or merge a backup into what's already on the phone.
  • Export a note, a selection, a notebook or everything as PDF, Markdown, a web page or plain text.
  • Import text and Markdown files, including a zip of Markdown folders.
Optional AI

AI if you want it, with your own key

Seer Notes has no AI built in and sends nothing on its own. Add a free or paid key from Google Gemini, xAI Grok or Groq to turn these on:

  • Summarise, fix grammar, rewrite, change the tone or turn bullet points into paragraphs. You see the result first, and nothing changes until you tap Apply.
  • Turn a meeting recording into a summary, decisions and action items, and add them to Today.
  • Transcribe recordings with AI when on-device speech to text can't, often for Urdu.
  • Ask questions about your notes, with numbered sources. Only the few passages that match are sent, and you can see which.
  • Make flashcards and quizzes from a note.
  • Smart search that finds notes by meaning (Gemini), off until you turn it on.
  • Journal AI: a weekly reflection and personal prompts, only if you switch it on.

With no key, no AI buttons appear at all. Your key is stored encrypted on your phone and never goes into backups. Vault notes and health records are never sent.

Questions

How do I install it?

On your Android phone, tap Download for Android. Open the downloaded file and, if Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. To update later, open Settings → Check for updates in the app. Updates install over the top and keep your notes.

Is Seer Notes free?

Yes. No ads and no account. AI features use your own key from Gemini, Grok or Groq. Some have free tiers, and any costs are between you and that provider.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. You only need a connection to back up to Google Drive or a WebDAV server, to use AI, or to load a link preview.

What happens if I lose my phone?

If you set up a backup, install Seer Notes on your new phone and restore it with your backup password. Without a backup, your notes stay on the old phone only. That's why the app reminds you to set one up.

Can you read my notes?

No. We have no servers and no account system, so your notes never reach us.

Does it work in Urdu?

Yes. The app can be fully in Urdu, Urdu notes use Nastaliq script, and voice typing and journal prompts support Urdu too. On-device Urdu speech recognition depends on your phone.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer.

Is Health records medical advice?

No. It's a private place to keep your own records of visits, medicines and prescriptions. Always follow your doctor's advice.
