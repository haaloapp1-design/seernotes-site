Privacy policy

Seer Notes · Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: your notes stay on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Backups go only to storage you choose, and AI runs only when you add your own key and tap an AI button.

Seer Notes is a notes and journal app for Android. It is built so that your notes stay on your phone. We (the developer) do not run any servers for the app, do not have accounts, and never receive your notes or any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Seer Notes has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting that sends data to us. We cannot see what you write.

Where your notes are stored

  • Notes, journal entries, photos, recordings, scans, habits, health records and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Notes you move to the Private Vault are encrypted on your phone. Only someone who can unlock the vault on your phone can read them.
  • Health records (doctor visits, medicines, prescription photos) are kept the same way: on your phone and in backups you make. They are never sent to an AI provider, never shown in search outside Health records, widgets or notifications (a follow-up reminder shows only "Follow-up visit" and a name).
  • If you uninstall the app without a backup, your notes are deleted with it.

Backups you choose

You can back up to storage you own: your Google Drive, a WebDAV server (such as Nextcloud) or a folder on your phone or SD card. Backups go straight from your phone to the place you pick. They can be encrypted with a password only you know.

  • Google Drive: if you connect it, the app asks Google for permission to use a hidden app folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata scope). The app can only see files it created there. It cannot see your other Drive files. Your Google account email is shown in the app so you know which account is connected. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • WebDAV: the server address and sign-in you enter are stored encrypted on your phone (Android Keystore) and are only sent to that server.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional AI features

Seer Notes has no AI service of its own. AI features appear only if you add your own API key from Google Gemini, xAI (Grok) or Groq. Your key is stored encrypted on your phone and is never included in backups.

  • Text is sent to the AI provider you chose only when you tap an AI button (for example "Summarise") or turn on an AI setting that clearly says what it sends (such as Smart search or Journal AI).
  • The text goes directly from your phone to that provider. It does not pass through us. The provider's own privacy policy applies to it.
  • Private Vault notes are never sent to AI. Locked journal entries are not sent unless you allow AI for the journal.

Other internet use

  • Link previews: when you paste a web link into a note, the app may load that page to show its title and picture. Only the link's own website is contacted.
  • On-device features: text recognition, document scanning, handwriting recognition and speech to text run on your phone using Google's on-device components. Android may download language packs for them.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record voice notes, only when you start a recording.
  • Camera / photos: to add photos and scan documents, only when you choose to.
  • Notifications and alarms: to show the reminders you set.
  • Fingerprint / face: to unlock the app or the vault. Your biometric data never leaves Android's secure hardware.

Children

Seer Notes is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete notes in the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything on your phone. Backups you made are in your own storage and you can delete them there. For Google Drive, open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → Seer Notes → Delete hidden app data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
